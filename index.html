Academic Regulations

Important academic rules and guidelines presented using HTML5 native expandable sections.

1. Attendance Requirements & Condonation

Students should follow the attendance requirements prescribed by the applicable academic regulations.

  • Minimum attendance requirements may apply for semester examinations.
  • Medical or other permitted condonation procedures may require supporting documents.
  • Students should refer to the latest official university regulations.
2. Continuous Internal Evaluation

Continuous Internal Evaluation may include internal examinations, quizzes, assignments, presentations and other academic activities.

3. Fee Refund Policy

Fee refund procedures are subject to the applicable university rules and regulatory requirements.

4. Student Code of Conduct

Students are expected to follow institutional policies, academic integrity requirements and campus conduct guidelines.
